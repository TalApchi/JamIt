Strongest MVP direction

Resonance Ladder is the most practical MVP base. It preserves the instrument metaphor, keeps the scale order obvious, makes the root note unmistakable, and gives both thumbs a natural rhythm without adding explanation.

Strongest long-term product direction

Breath Halo is the most ownable long-term direction. It teaches tonal center visually, looks unlike a conventional app, and can grow into gestures, session presence, reactive light, pressure, and expressive sound behavior.
